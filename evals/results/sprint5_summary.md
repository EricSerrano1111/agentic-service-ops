## Disclosures (verbatim where quoted)

1. ADR-094's sentence: "In a real deployment, evaluation sets would come from real user questions and subject-matter experts. Here they were generated by an independent model under a written brief; the owner reviewed the brief and spot-checked the ambiguous labels."
2. "The sets were generated, not owner-written." (The golden set is the exception: 10 of its 36 items are owner-written, ADR-074.)
3. **The persona-drop selection effect (L-86, update 2026-10-11):** "The owner's drops at the skim were made on persona grounds: a question a dispatcher or call-centre agent would ask, but not a senior operations leader with an overview of the business unit (the brief's stated asker). Labels were not changed, and the owner had not seen any system output. Five of the 31 skimmed rows were dropped (all `ambiguous`). Persona drops may remove the more granular, operational questions, which leaves the sets weighted toward broad leadership questions."
4. **L-92: the interpretation gate does not test declines.** The frozen pairs hold 1 decline pair among 23 correct pairs, and the failure kind that sank `qa_interp_v1`, `v2` and `v3` is a correct decline judged unfaithful. A pass would not have shown the check handles declines, and the failure does not measure how often it rejects them.
5. The generator and the system's models are both Gemini (L-86), so easy phrasing may inflate the routing figures.
